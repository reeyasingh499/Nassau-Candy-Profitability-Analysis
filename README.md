# 🍬 Nassau Candy Distributor - Product Line Profitability & Margin Analysis

An end-to-end data analysis and interactive dashboard project built to analyze product profitability, division performance, and gross margins for Nassau Candy Distributor.

---

## 📊 Executive Summary & Key Insights

1. *Chocolate Division Dominance*: The Chocolate division generates the highest revenue and gross profit compared to Sugar and Other divisions.
2. *High-Margin Key Drivers: Products like *Wonka Bar - Scrumdiddlyumptious and Wonka Bar - Nutty Crunch Surprise yield exceptional profit margins (>69%).
3. *Low-Performing SKUs*: Products in the Sugar and Other categories (e.g., Fun Dip, Nerds) exhibit lower margins and unit sales, presenting opportunities for pricing restructuring or inventory optimization.

---

- *Language*: Python 3.x
